======
Citing
======

Using OptiX in published work requires citing:

   J. J. Esteve-Paredes, M. A. García-Blázquez, A. J. Uría-Álvarez, M. Camarasa-Gómez and J. J. Palacios,
   *Excitons in nonlinear optical responses: shift current in MoS2 and GeS monolayers*,
   npj Computational Materials **11**, 13 (2025).
   `doi:10.1038/s41524-024-01504-2 <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41524-024-01504-2>`_

.. code-block:: bibtex

   @article{EsteveParedes2025,
     author  = {Esteve-Paredes, J. J. and Garc{\'i}a-Bl{\'a}zquez, M. A. and Ur{\'i}a-{\'A}lvarez, A. J.
                and Camarasa-G{\'o}mez, M. and Palacios, J. J.},
     title   = {Excitons in nonlinear optical responses: shift current in {MoS2} and {GeS} monolayers},
     journal = {npj Computational Materials},
     volume  = {11},
     pages   = {13},
     year    = {2025},
     doi     = {10.1038/s41524-024-01504-2}
   }

If you used exciton states from Xatu, please also cite:

   A. J. Uría-Álvarez, J. J. Esteve-Paredes, M. A. García-Blázquez and J. J. Palacios,
   *Efficient computation of optical excitations in two-dimensional materials with the Xatu code*,
   Computer Physics Communications **295**, 109001 (2024).
   `doi:10.1016/j.cpc.2023.109001 <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpc.2023.109001>`_
